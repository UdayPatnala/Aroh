## 1. Document Versioning & Audit Policy

Every legal notice, terms document, and privacy policy within AROH is managed under strict cryptographic and semantic versioning. Material changes to any policy require:
1. Increment of the minor or major document version (e.g., `v1.0.0` -> `v1.1.0` or `v2.0.0`).
2. Immediate entry in this central audit register.
3. Advance notification to registered users where required by law.
4. Prompt re-consent evaluation if purpose or data categories change materially.
